# Project idea

## Concept
AURELLE & CO. is a premium fashion and lifestyle storefront concept built around thoughtful, enduring personal style.

## Problem
Fashion landing pages can feel crowded, transactional, or visually inconsistent. This project explores how a clear editorial story can help visitors discover a small assortment without sacrificing useful shopping cues.

## Target audience
Style-conscious shoppers looking for considered clothing, jewelry, bags, fragrance, watches, shoes, and accessories, especially people who value quality and simplicity.

## Objectives
- Create an elegant, responsive first impression.
- Make products easy to browse and search.
- Demonstrate a useful frontend-only bag and wishlist.
- Clearly distinguish sample content from real reviews or business services.

## Proposed solution
A single-page storefront pairs large lifestyle imagery and calm typography with product/category browsing, persistent demo selections, FAQs, and validated contact/newsletter forms. It is a static concept, not a live retail or payment service.
