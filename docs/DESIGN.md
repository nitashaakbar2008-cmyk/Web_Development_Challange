# Design direction

## Philosophy
An editorial, quiet-luxury presentation lets imagery and considered typography lead. Functional controls stay visible and familiar; whitespace and fine rules separate content instead of heavy cards or decoration.

## Palette
- Warm ivory — `#FAF8F3`
- Soft cream — `#F2EDE4`
- Champagne beige — `#E6D7C3`
- Muted blush — `#EAD8D2`
- Sage — `#A7AA91`
- Warm charcoal — `#292724`
- White — `#FFFFFF`

## Typography
Playfair Display is used for expressive editorial headings and product names; DM Sans is used for navigation, body copy, form controls, and small labels. Georgia and Arial provide local fallbacks.

## UI/UX principles
- Use concise labels, meaningful alt text, keyboard-visible focus, and semantic landmarks.
- Mark sample reviews as illustrative and explain that forms and checkout do not connect to a service.
- Keep product controls usable on touch screens and provide readable empty states.
- Respect the user's reduced-motion preference.

## Layout and spacing
Content uses a centered 1320px maximum width with generous section spacing. Product and category grids reduce columns at tablet and phone widths; the navigation becomes a collapsible mobile menu.

## Inspiration references
The visual language draws on premium fashion editorials and restrained lifestyle catalogues: large photographic compositions, muted natural colors, refined serif headlines, and understated product details. No specific website assets are reproduced.

## Responsive approach
CSS grid and flexible sizing adapt from six category columns and four products across on wide screens to two columns on compact screens. Navigation, typography, section spacing, image crops, forms, and footer layout are adjusted at tablet/mobile breakpoints.
